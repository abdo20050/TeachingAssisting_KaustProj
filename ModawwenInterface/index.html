The Hinderance of Taking Notes:

In classrooms nowadays, students are getting distracted from what teachers are saying because they're busy taking lots of notes. While teachers try to make lessons interesting, students can get so caught up in writing everything down and translating that they miss out on the real interaction happening in the class.
